OUR APPROACH

Start with the work.
Follow through to operation.

01 /

Understand the work

Get close to the equipment and the people operating and maintaining it. Spend time alongside them doing the job, observing the friction, and understanding what causes it. Work out together what an optimized solution looks like in real operating conditions.

02 /

Develop the solution

Shape the controls, communications, and software around that understanding. Choose technology to suit the work, rather than forcing the work to fit the technology.

03 /

Prove it in operation

Work alongside the people operating and maintaining the equipment to bring the system online and into production. Test behaviour, work through faults, and make sure the system delivers the improvements that matter in practice.

ABOUT SHEPHERD

Practical understanding. Technical depth.

We are forward deployed practitioners with extensive hands on experience across a wide range of industries. Our experience comes from seeing systems work, seeing them fail, and working through the details to understand why.

We help equipment owners and machine builders turn ideas, operational requirements, and difficult technical problems into working machines, processes, and connected systems.

Good systems should handle complexity without passing it on to the person doing the job. They should work quietly in the background, provide useful information when attention is needed, and remain understandable to the people responsible for maintaining them.
